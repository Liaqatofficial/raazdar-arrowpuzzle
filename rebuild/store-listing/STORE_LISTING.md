## Long description
```
Arrow Puzzle by Raazdar is a clean, modern take on the arrow logic puzzle genre. Every arrow points in one of four directions — up, right, down, or left. Tap an arrow and it flies off the board in the direction it faces, but only if its path to the edge is completely clear. Blocked by another arrow in the way? The tapped arrow shakes in place, refusing to leave. Clear every arrow to finish the level.

Arrow Puzzle is designed for offline play: the full puzzle set ships inside the app, so you can solve puzzles on the subway, in a plane, or anywhere without Wi-Fi or data. No account is required to play, and progress is stored on your device.

• Tricky arrow logic — every tap counts. One blocked arrow can cost you the run.
• Challenging puzzle game that grows harder as you progress through the level set.
• Brain teaser mechanic — plan the order in which arrows leave the board.
• Strategic puzzle play — clear jammed clusters step by step.
• Classic arrow escape gameplay, modernized with smooth visuals and responsive controls.
• Offline puzzle game — play fully offline, no account needed.

Arrow Puzzle by Raazdar is an ongoing project: more levels, improved usability, and tuned difficulty crafting may arrive in updates. Playing is free and does not require registration.
```
