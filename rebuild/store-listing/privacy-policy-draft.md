Raazdar ("we") developed the Arrow Puzzle app as a services-free app in which there are no subscriptions for a service and the app stores all puzzle progress and settings **only on your device**.

## 1. Information we collect
The Arrow Puzzle app does not require an account to play and, in its services configuration, does not use third-party analytics tool, collection of advertising identifiers, or tracking.

## 2. Data stored on your device
The app stores on your device: your progress (levels completed), your app settings (sound, haptic feedback), and content needed to run the game, all in local app storage. Deleting the app removes this data.

## 3. Permissions
The app requests only the permissions it needs to function (e.g. vibration for haptic feedback, notifications if you opt in to reminders). It requests no access to your photos, contacts, microphone, camera, or precise location.

## 4. Children
The app does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Questions about this policy: contact via the details published at https://raazdar.xyz.
